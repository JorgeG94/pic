pending_t

type, public :: pending_t

Bytes of an escape sequence that arrived without their continuation.

A terminal is free to split ESC [ A across two reads, and a decoder that forgot the ESC between calls would report a stray escape and then the letter A. This carries the fragment across.

      !! A terminal is free to split `ESC [ A` across two reads, and a decoder
