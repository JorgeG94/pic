public interface next_poisson_int

Draw a Poisson-distributed count with mean mean_milli/1000.

The mean is given a thousand times over so that fractional means are expressible without a real type: mean_milli = 2500 means 2.5 events.

Counts the exponential inter-arrivals that fit inside a fixed window, which is the defining construction of a Poisson process rather than an approximation of one. Cost is O(mean).

mean_milli must be in [0, POISSON_MAX_MEAN_MILLI]. Zero returns zero without consuming a draw. Out of range returns zero and, if err is present, sets ERROR_VALIDATION.
