public interface next_exponential_int

Draw an exponentially distributed integer with the given mean, in whatever unit mean is expressed in.

Sampling is a table lookup and two integer multiplies. EXP_TABLE holds the inverse CDF -ln(1 - k/4096) in Q32.32 fixed point, generated once by tools/autogen/gen_exp_table.py with 60-digit decimal arithmetic. Twelve random bits choose a cell, thirty-one more interpolate linearly inside it, and the result is scaled by mean.

The tail

The top cell runs to infinity and cannot be interpolated into. It is handled with the memorylessness of the exponential instead: if U is uniform on the top cell then -ln(1 - U) is distributed as ln(4096) plus a fresh exponential, so the sampler adds ln(4096) and draws again. That is exact, and it costs an extra draw with probability 1/4096.

Capping the top entry at a finite value and interpolating into it – the obvious alternative – biases the mean by 0.147%. This scheme leaves 0.002%, and all of that residue is linear interpolation inside the body cells, not the tail.

mean must be in [0, EXPONENTIAL_MAX_MEAN]. A mean of zero always returns zero without consuming a draw. Out of range returns zero and, if err is present, sets ERROR_VALIDATION.
