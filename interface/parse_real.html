public interface parse_real

Parse a string into a real, reporting failures through error_t.

The accepted grammar is optional surrounding whitespace, an optional sign, a mantissa holding at least one digit and at most one decimal point (so both ".5" and "5." are accepted, "." is not), and an optional exponent introduced by e, E, d or D with an optional sign and at least one digit.

Everything else is rejected, in particular the spellings that a list directed read would happily accept: "nan", "inf", "infinity", repeat counts such as "3*7", comma separated values such as "1,2", and any trailing junk. Values that overflow the requested kind are rejected as well instead of becoming infinity.

Specific procedures exist for sp and dp.
